Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university focuses on education, research and innovation, particularly in marine science, environmental studies and technology.

About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia. It was established in 1979 at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, to provide practical training facilities for students and research facilities for lecturers.

In June 1996, the institution became Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, the Malaysian government approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) in 1999. It was subsequently renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was upgraded to a full-fledged public university and officially became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT). Today, UMT offers various academic programmes and continues to focus on marine science, environmental sustainability, technology and research.
